# 🎖️ VDP, CERT-In, DoD & YesWeHack Programs

> **Workspace Folders:** `vdp/`, `certin/`, `dod/`, `yeswehack/`

---

## 🎯 Program Categories

### 1. CERT-In (`certin/`)
- Indian Computer Emergency Response Team
- Scope: Critical infrastructure, government portals, educational institutes
- Objective: Responsible disclosure of high/critical vulnerabilities, SQLi, sensitive data leakage

### 2. US Department of Defense (`dod/`)
- Platform: HackerOne DoD VDP
- Scope: `*.mil` assets, defense web portals
- Key Vulns: Open redirects, information disclosure, unauthenticated panels, CVE templates

### 3. YesWeHack (`yeswehack/`)
- European & Global private/public bounty programs
- Focus: Business logic errors, GraphQL endpoints, authorization bypass
