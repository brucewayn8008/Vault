## SuperX (superx.so) — competitor snapshot (report dated Sep 2026)
- Web app + Chrome extension for X creators; ~$20k MRR, 478 active subs (Stripe-verified), growth flattened from 20–25% MoM to ~3%.
- Pricing: Pro $49, Advanced $49 launch ($99 list), Ultra $199; no free tier, trial only.
- Moat = **distribution** (founder audience, daily build-in-public video, 40% lifetime affiliates, 30+ free SEO tools), not features.
- Biggest risk: X API pay-per-use pricing + reply restrictions (Feb/Apr 2026).
